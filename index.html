Evaluator photo

A passport-style photo. This page checks it as you take it, so you only submit a good one. Your photo is uploaded only after it passes every check.

  • 🙂
    FaceLook straight at the camera, neutral expression, mouth closed, eyes open. No sunglasses, mask or cap. Remove anything covering your forehead.
  • 💡
    LightFace a window or bright light so your face is evenly lit. Never stand with the light behind you.
  • 🧱
    Background (strict)Stand in front of a bare, plain wall, light coloured if you can. Nothing behind you: no door, switch, curtain, shelf, window or poster. Nobody else in the frame. Wear your Spinny uniform. The camera will not take the photo until the wall is plain.
  • 📱
    PhoneAsk a colleague to take it with the back camera: phone upright at your eye level, about 1–1.5 m away. Selfies are not allowed (they distort the face).
